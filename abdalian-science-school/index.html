The school

One method,
three steps.

no shortcuts, no tricks — just a timetable that works

Exp. 01

Live where you learn.

Abdalian is a boarding school. Students live on campus in air-conditioned hostels, so no time goes to the daily commute and evenings go to study.

Exp. 02

Science, and only science.

Every student here is in a science group: Matric Science, then FSc Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering or General Science. The whole staff room teaches the same kind of student.

Exp. 03

Discipline, measured.

Strict routine is the school's own promise. The board's figures back it up: 1,129 of the 1,183 Matric candidates it sent across seven reported years passed.

Boarding life

A weekday at
Abdalian.*

Air-conditioned classrooms, air-conditioned hostels, and a day with a shape to it. Scroll through the timetable.

05:00AM
Fajr & wake-up
  1. 05:00

    Fajr & wake-up

    Prayer, a short walk around the grounds, and a tidy room before breakfast.

  2. 07:00

    Breakfast

    Everyone eats together in the mess, then heads to class.

  3. 07:45

    Classes

    Lectures and practicals in air-conditioned classrooms, through the Muzaffargarh summer.

  4. 13:30

    Lunch & rest

    Zuhr, lunch, and a short rest. Nobody learns well on no sleep.

  5. 15:30

    Supervised prep

    Homework and revision with teachers on hand. Doubts get cleared the same day.

  6. 17:30

    Games

    Cricket, football and fresh air before Maghrib.

  7. 20:00

    Evening study

    Quiet hall study after dinner, with tests through the week.

  8. 22:30

    Lights out

    A fixed bedtime, so tomorrow starts on time too.

How to apply*

Four steps
to a seat.

  1. Call or WhatsApp

    Ask about seats, fees and hostel availability for your class and group.

  2. Visit the campus

    Come with a parent or guardian. See the classrooms and hostels, and meet the staff.

  3. Submit the form

    Bring the B-Form, your last result card or DMC, and passport-size photographs.

  4. Confirm your seat

    Pay the admission and hostel dues, collect the joining date, and pack your bags.

* General process. Please confirm the exact steps and documents with the admissions office.
